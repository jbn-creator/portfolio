Predicting Power-Grid Outages with Deep Learning Supervised by Dr Shirin Dora Ongoing

Using deep learning to predict power-grid outages. Reliable early forecasting matters because it lets operators take preemptive action such as switching to backup systems, redirecting load, or simply preparing. Battlefield RL simulation of global geopolitics Ongoing

Battlefield is a multi-agent reinforcement learning environment where countries act as agents competing over resources: capital, land, people, oil under constraints that loosely model international competition. The project explores what strategies emerge when agents with different priors and reward weightings interact in a shared zero-sum economy.

The motivating question came from listening to Jiang Xuequin's game-theoretic analyses of geopolitics: if states really were independent agents maximising under a zero-sum game, what would optimal play look like, and would it resemble anything we recognise? The honest answer is probably no since the environment is far too simple to model real strategic behaviour. But the simpler question is interesting in itself: what does multi-agent RL converge to under these constraints, and how do those equilibria shift as you change the resource structure or the reward shape if they do at all?
